1. CORPUS OVERVIEW
This digital time-capsule preserves 16 documented and reconstructed generations
across England (Dorset & Somerset), Ireland (Co. Tipperary & Waterford),
Nigeria (Emure-Ekiti, Ijebu-Owo, Benin City, Ondo & Ibadan), Newfoundland
(Bonavista, Greenspond, Hillview, Herring Neck, Twillingate, Tilt Cove,
Lushes Bight, Burlington & Smiths Harbour), Maine, and Ontario (Sudbury &
Toronto), anchored by 45 dated events, 40 archival series, and 37 WGS84 loci.

2. MULTI-CENTURY FILE INVENTORY & OPEN STANDARDS
- index.html : Zero-dependency interactive web atlas (inlined CSS, Leaflet 1.9.4,
  and Natural Earth 1:50m offline world vector map). Opens in any web browser.
- Family_History_Archive_1475_2026.xml : TEI-XML P5 scholarly edition encoding
  diplomatic transcriptions, <listBibl> sources, <listPlace> WGS84 coordinates,
  and three-tier Genealogical Proof Standard certainty attributes (high/medium/low).
- Ancestral_Places_WGS84.geojson : RFC 7946 / Linked Places GIS FeatureCollection
  of all 37 ancestral sites, the Sept. 1544 Arundell three-parish manor polygon,
  and 4 migration routes in [longitude, latitude] order for QGIS / ArcGIS.
- Kolade_Squires_Lush_1475_2026.ged : GEDCOM 7.0 UTF-8 lineage-linked pedigree
  spanning Generations 16 to 1 with embedded @S01@-@S40@ archival citations.
- Family_History_Timeline_1475_2026.csv : Flat UTF-8 comma-separated dataset.
- Family_History_Data_1475_2026.json : Complete structured JSON data spine.
- Dossiers_PDFA/ : ISO 19005-2 PDF/A-2u editions of Volumes I–III, Master
  Bibliographies, Allied Supporting-Family Gazetteers, and the Ogun Monograph.

3. EVIDENTIARY GRADING (GENEALOGICAL PROOF STANDARD)
- DIRECT (cert='high') : Filial or vital link stated in primary contemporary records.
- INDIRECT (cert='medium') : Sealed across register gaps via three-lives copyhold
  reversion, negative probate/oath screens, cadastral tenure, or tonal philology,
  plus the bounded two-candidate 1775 Motcombe paternity fork.
- CANDIDATE (cert='low') : Working archival leads reserved for physical strongroom
  checks (1846 Fethard Philip Dwyer lead & three-model Ojomu Ogedu succession).
